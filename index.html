About The Platform

What is our platform?

Multi-Doctor Appointment Conflict Scheduler is a smart clinic appointment management platform that helps patients book appointments with doctors while preventing double-booking of doctors and consultation rooms.

Doctor Availability Safeguard

Verifies individual physician working hours and existing appointments across our 10 predefined clinic doctors to guarantee zero double-bookings.

Consultation Room Conflict Checks

Calculates room occupancies across medical specialties, ensuring diagnostic suites and examination rooms are never double-booked.

Earliest Next Available Slot Finder

If a requested slot is occupied, our algorithm scans up to 7 days forward to automatically present the earliest available slot for both doctor and room.

Key System Highlights

  • Checks doctor availability in real-time.
  • Checks consultation room availability.
  • Prevents overlapping time intervals.
  • Suggests next available appointment slots.
  • Predefined list of 10 registered clinic doctors.
  • Patients track live status & submit reviews.
